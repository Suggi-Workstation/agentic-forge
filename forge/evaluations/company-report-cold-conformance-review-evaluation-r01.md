## Findings

### The embedded packs and decisions are complete and internally reproducible

Independent extraction parsed both 1,617-word packs and both six-unit JSON
outputs. The two pack hashes match their 12,293 embedded bytes. Reader A's
1,010-word output hash matches its 10,133 content bytes without a terminal LF;
Reader B's 1,193-word output hash matches its 11,666 bytes with a terminal LF.
All required unit fields are present, unit identifiers are unique, and each
output independently recomputes three PASS and three HALT decisions.[1]

The counterbalanced decisions also match the displayed summary: both readers
HALTed the three original units and PASSed the three corrected controls. Neither
corrected-control record contains an unsupported added finding. Reader A and
Reader B both remove the market-only retained-capital pass without forcing a
score change, reject the completed smooth DCF, and reject the two positive
liquidity classifications.[1]

The inconsistent terminal-LF hash boundary is a documentation ambiguity, not a
content blocker: each declared digest matches one explicit extraction boundary
and each JSON parses. A revision should state the byte boundary for every hash,
but no semantic result turns on the final newline.[1][5]

keeps decisive unknowns visible. The company template treats a supported

### The decisive answer-key-blind boundary is not preserved

The research makes the blind pair material to its conclusion, but it embeds
only the reader instructions, packs, outputs, and an author-written summary of
the tool traces. It does not embed or link either reader's complete tool-access
receipt, delegated context receipt, or exact trace bytes. The available records
therefore do not let an evaluator verify which files, prior messages, or tool
results each reader actually received or opened.[1]

This is not a ceremonial trace request. Reader B's output says its review was
limited to the pack and four contracts, while the research author reports that
Reader B also loaded the generic `write-evaluation` skill. The extra skill
contained no identified answer key and does not itself show contamination, but
the contradiction proves that the reader's own limitation statement and the
prompt prohibition cannot establish actual context compliance. Reader A's
claimed target-only boundary likewise rests on the unpreserved trace summary.[1]

The current high-confidence Forge lesson already distinguishes raw output from
evidence for blind timing and separate-reader claims. Brain prior work similarly
requires evidence attached to the exact reviewed artifact and warns that a
fresh review node is not automatically independent assurance. Source-
verification practice requires a traceable evidence ledger and tests of source
dependence, not acceptance of an author's summary of the verification path.[5][6]

Without the execution receipts, both displayed classifications remain usable as
semantic analyses, but the decisive statement that two official readers were
answer-key-blind is unknown. That blocks ADVANCE because the root idea made the
blind-pair result an affirmative threshold and the evaluation template forbids
advancement over missing material evidence.[2][5]

### A bounded correction is preferable to closure

The evidence does not support REJECT. The packs, outputs, current rules, and
arithmetic make the claimed classification capability plausible, and the
missing receipts could change only whether the root idea's required blind
comparison is established. The gap is specific, testable, and within the first
corrective cycle.[1][2]

The revised research must:

1. Embed or link immutable, hashed execution receipts for both official reader
   passes. Each receipt must preserve the exact delegated prompt/context, every
   file or skill read, tool-call order, start/end command evidence, saved output
   identity, and any parent-supplied material. A reader self-report or parent
   trace summary is not a receipt.
2. If either original receipt is unavailable, run a new predeclared pair and
   label it a new pass. Preserve the complete receipts before grading. Use a
   strict target-only context or explain and test every extra context source;
   do not silently repair the existing records.
3. State the exact byte boundary for every fixture and output hash, including
   whether a terminal LF is part of the hashed object.
4. Recompute the blind-pair result from the preserved records and retain the
   existing limits: curated retrospective passages, same model family, unknown
   historical author-side execution, unknown focus-packet preparation cost, no
   causal future-error claim, and no permanent-gate recommendation.

A fresh evaluation may ADVANCE only if the receipts establish both blind
contexts and the paired result still passes. Any later proposal must be limited
to a reversible, predeclared prospective trial that measures misses, false
HALTs, unsupported scope expansion, author and reviewer time, packet assembly,
and revision outcomes. If the traces show answer-key exposure or a rerun fails a
material control, the next evaluation should stop or narrow the idea rather
than force a proposal.[1][2][6]

## Verdict and Handoff

**Verdict: REVISE. Exact next stage: `research`. This verdict uses corrective
cycle 1 of 2.**

The research preserves unusually strong fixtures and separate outputs, and the
bounded classifications survive independent rule and arithmetic checks. It does
not preserve the execution evidence needed to verify its decisive answer-key-
blind pair. Reader B's disclosed extra skill read and contradictory output
limitation make that missing evidence material rather than formal.[1][5]

The pipeline returns to research for the four corrections above. No proposal,
Investing Hub report edit, framework change, permanent review gate,
implementation, approval, or security recommendation is authorized.

Confidence in REVISE is high. The embedded record structure, hashes under stated
newline boundaries, current-rule classifications, and material arithmetic are
directly reproducible, while the absence of linked reader execution receipts
and the Reader B contradiction are direct properties of the target.[1][3][5]
Confidence would fall if a resolving immutable source already linked by the
target supplied both complete receipts and established their exact context
boundaries. No such source is linked or present in the reviewed chain.
