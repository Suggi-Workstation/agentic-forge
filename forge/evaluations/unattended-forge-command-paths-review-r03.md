## Findings

### The purpose-separated gate closes the prior false-positive path

The r03 claim is expressly limited to simple extraction, fixed arithmetic, and
ASCII rejection. Each positive purpose now has its own route-neutral prompt,
fixture, cold control and treatment traces, first-substantive-route label,
exact-result check, and purpose-level disposition. The structural case has a
separate non-equivalence classifier and cannot contribute to the incremental
selection-benefit result.[1]

The positive-purpose classifier distinguishes `permitted-equivalent-first`,
`denied-first`, and `other`. Each purpose passes only when all three treatments
are permitted and equivalent first, no treatment contains a later denied
attempt, every control and treatment reaches the exact result without weaker
verification, at least two controls are denied first, no control is `other`, and
all raw traces and grader records exist. The overall candidate requires all
three positive purposes, the structural gate, every negative case, repository
checks, and review; there is no run-level label or averaging across purposes.[1]

The r02 review's counterexample no longer passes this contract. A safe extraction
choice cannot label an arithmetic or ASCII trace because every trace contains
one purpose. A denied first route fails that purpose, while a denied route after
an acceptable first route independently fails the treatment. Exact output
cannot override either trajectory failure. This is a logical inspection of the
written gate, not a claim that the comparison has run.[1][2]

These controls fit the independently checked evaluation guidance. A result
contract must identify the model, harness, environment, tools, budgets, trial
rule, grader, and aggregation method; aggregate success must not hide a failing
task slice. Outcome and trajectory verdicts answer different questions and
should remain separate when the route itself affects safety or the claimed
benefit.[6]

### Passed wording, authority, and evidence boundaries remain intact

The proposed paragraph is unchanged from r02. It applies only when the active
unattended approval context is in `deny` mode, makes the returned guard result
authoritative, prohibits retry, rephrasing, or configuration changes, and
forbids an alternate route when the result says not to attempt the same outcome.
Capability classes and equivalence precede conditional current-VPS examples;
read/search value exposure remains explicitly non-equivalent to a required
machine mismatch status.[1][2]

The checked platform contract supports these boundaries. Current official
security documentation distinguishes unattended `deny`, which blocks instantly
and requires another path, from unattended `approve`; it also states that
hardline and user-defined deny rules apply before ordinary approval bypasses.[7]
At the proposal's pinned source revision, active unattended contexts are ordered
and resolved by their configured mode, `execute_code` in the first active deny
context is blocked before execution, and unconditional floors are evaluated
before ordinary unattended handling.[9][10] Official code-execution guidance
supplies only the general programmatic-tool versus shell-command distinction,
not the proposed Forge-specific map.[8]

The proposal does not claim that prose enforces runtime security, that the named
utilities are portable or generally safe, that historical commands were
reconstructed, that the paragraph has changed behavior, or that time, token,
tool-call, or economic savings were measured. Implementation remains separate
work after human approval.[1][3]

### Feasibility, negative cases, worst failure, and reversal pass

The target declares twelve matched pairs and 24 cold contexts across four
purpose-specific cases. It freezes prompts, fixtures, expected results,
capability classes, model, system context, harness, tools, permissions, approval
mode, sampling settings, budgets, pair order, classifiers, and dispositions.
Control and treatment differ only by the prior versus candidate protocol within
a pair; raw traces remain separate; seeded and unseeded variation receive
predeclared interpretations; opened results cannot be rerun, excluded,
relabeled, or repaired.[1]

The absent-utility checks prohibit installation, policy changes, unsupported
interpreter fallback, and success without an equivalent result. The isolated
regression fixtures cover an allowed or approve context, recoverable denial,
stricter denial, unconditional floor, and unrelated scope. The repository gate
limits the substantive diff to the exact paragraph and requires ASCII, staged
diff, ID, and final human-review checks.[1]

The worst failure is correctly identified as an aggregate PASS despite a denied
route or weaker check in a covered purpose. Separate prompts, purpose-specific
classifiers, fail-on-any-treatment-denial, exact-result requirements, and an
all-purpose aggregate gate address that false positive. The returned-result
rule and stricter-denial fixtures separately address bypass risk. Deleting the
one paragraph is a complete repository rollback with no data migration or
runtime reversal.[1][6][9][10]

The comparison and regression package remains unexecuted; first-choice benefit,
recovery cost, portability, and behavior under run variation remain unknown.
These are disclosed limits rather than READY blockers because the proposal
makes successful execution of every gate a precondition to adoption and treats
failure or inconclusive evidence as no adoption. No research claim is expanded
to fill those gaps.[1][2]

## Learning Decision

`LEARNINGS.md` remains unchanged.[5]

- **Selection:** Mapping each claimed purpose to a falsifiable classifier before
  drafting the first proposal would have exposed the combined-run gap earlier.
- **Evidence and test design:** Purpose-specific prompts, independent route
  labels, a fail-on-any-treatment-denial rule, and separate outcome and
  trajectory checks make the written result contract complete.
- **Process:** The correction handoff was exact and no new tool, template, or
  stage failure affected this review.
- **Repetition:** The existing claimed-result-contract lesson already requires
  every benefit clause to map to an observable predicate and negative case.
- **Coverage:** This revision is an unexecuted application inside the same
  pipeline that exposed the gap. It is neither an independent third pipeline nor
  evidence that applying the lesson improved an executed outcome, so no new
  lesson, confidence increase, or governance permission passes the admission
  gate.[5]
