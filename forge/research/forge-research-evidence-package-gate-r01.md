# Research: Conditional Evidence-Package Gate

## Question and Method

This report tests the root idea's exact question: does one conditional research-
template rule add decision-relevant classification over a fair semantic reading
of the current research contract, while accepting complete packages and ordinary
source synthesis and routing an immaterial unsupported reader result to claim
narrowing rather than a corrective cycle?[1]

At starting HEAD `b41f4bfca263809e3b61ca2f684d1fea1b4cc20d`, the board had one
unselected discovery awaiting human review and this valid `research` row. The
root idea was authored by Analyst, so Researcher satisfies the separate-author
research gate. The worktree was clean; the current idea, method memory,
research template, research skill, board, protocol, and logs were read before
substantive work. `LEARNINGS.md` remained read-only.[1][2][8][11]

Before opening the target reports or their evaluations, the following
provisional explanation was recorded in task scratch: the current template
already requires checks actually performed and reproducible source checks to be
documented, but it does not enumerate the records needed when a procedural
comparison or independent-reader result materially supports a conclusion. A
single conditional line might make that boundary easier to apply. It adds
value only if a fair reading of the current contract misses a material defect
that the candidate catches, without blocking complete packages, ordinary source
synthesis, or an independently decidable negative finding.

The predeclared disconfirmation conditions were equal classifications under the
current and candidate gates, a false blocker on a corrected or ordinary control,
reader disagreement, inability to route the retained-earnings case to claim
narrowing, or wording that required more than one checklist item and at most one
skill sentence.[1]

The full-semantic current baseline was frozen as follows:

> Apply the current research template and research skill as a complete contract,
> not as a word-presence check. A report must document its method and checks
> actually performed; identify checked paths or URLs, passages or results, and
> limitations for important claims; keep consequential claims independently
> supported or explicitly unknown; document reproducible source checks; and
> avoid invented tests.

Both gates used these four outcomes:

- `package required`: a procedural comparison, reproduction claim, or separate-
  reader result materially supports the report, but the performed method,
  inputs, result records, or comparator application is not recoverable;
- `package complete`: recoverable rules or instrument, inputs, invocation,
  per-case results, and separate comparator or reader records support the
  material procedural result;
- `claim narrowing sufficient`: independently checked evidence decides the
  finding and the unsupported procedural result is withdrawn, marked
  unavailable, or given no decision weight; and
- `not applicable`: no material procedural comparison, reproduction, or reader-
  agreement claim needs such a package.

The candidate was frozen before grading:

> When a procedural comparison, reproduction claim, or independent-reader result
> materially supports a finding, preserve recoverable rules or instrument bytes,
> inputs or fixtures, environment and invocation, per-case raw results, and
> unmerged comparator or reader outputs. If the finding is independently
> decidable, explicitly withdraw the unsupported claim or give it no decision
> weight.

The scratch heading called this 49 words. A later fixed `wc -w` check returned
51 whitespace-delimited words; this report uses the measured count rather than
the incorrect provisional label. The rule names five record classes: instrument
or rules, inputs or fixtures, environment and invocation, per-case raw results,
and unmerged comparator or reader outputs.

The frozen corpus contained five Forge research reports and one ordinary source-
synthesis control:

- T1 and T2: the initial and corrected bounded-freshness research;
- T3 and T4: the initial and corrected claim-map research;
- T5: the retained-earnings report whose separate-reader records were not
  preserved but whose negative decision was later reproduced directly; and
- T6: the Brain's sourced agent-evaluation topic, which reports external studies
  rather than a new local procedural comparison.[3][4][5][6][7]

Two synthetic comprehension controls were also frozen. S1 claimed a material
18/20 two-reader result while declaring the rules, cases, per-case outputs, and
reader records unavailable. S2 described passage-cited primary-source synthesis
with one unresolved contradiction and no procedural or reader claim.

Two isolated same-model-family readers received the gates and target bodies but
not the evaluations, root idea, board, logs, or method memory. Reader A applied
the targets T1 through T6; Reader B used T6 through T1. Both applied the current
baseline to every target before the candidate. Reader A additionally loaded two
general Forge skill files despite the instruction to read only the targets; it
did not open an evaluation or target verdict. Reader B's tool record shows only
the six target reads. This is context separation and order balancing, not model
independence, and Reader A's extra general-skill exposure remains a limitation.

After both outputs were fixed, the five evaluations were opened as retrospective
comparison evidence. Their dispositions were not treated as infallible answer
keys. Reader classifications and concise preserved reasons are reported
separately below.

The candidate therefore changes 0 of 16 reader classifications relative to the
full current baseline. It catches the same two material historical package gaps,
passes the same two corrected packages, accepts both ordinary controls, and
catches the same synthetic defect. The current template already requires methods
and performed checks, reproducible source checks, important results, independent
support or explicit unknowns, and no invented tests. When that contract is read
semantically, the candidate's enumerated records make the rule easier to quote
but do not change any tested classification.[2]

The retained-earnings case is the decisive contradiction to the provisional
hypothesis. Its later evaluator directly reproduced the zero decision delta and
assigned the unavailable reader agreement no additional weight.[5] Neither cold
reader selected `claim narrowing sufficient` because the research artifact
itself does not explicitly withdraw the reader comparison or state that it has
no decision weight. The candidate requires that explicit sentence and therefore
still returns `package required` before evaluation. Used as a hard pre-write
gate, it would block an otherwise independently decidable negative report until
the author added narrowing language. That is a small edit, but this experiment
does not show a decision improvement over the evaluator's semantic treatment.

### Recoverability is supported; a new local line is not

The Forge's method memory already requires exact instruments, fixtures, raw
per-case outputs, separate reader records, and full-contract comparators.[8]
Brain prior work independently states that an agent-evaluation contract includes
the task and environment, harness, grader, protocol, and raw run artifacts, and
that a trace proves only the operations and fields its instrumentation records.[6][7]

ACM defines research artifacts to include software, scripts, input datasets, raw
data, and analysis scripts. Its artifact-evaluation criteria require sufficient
documentation, relevance, completeness, and exercisability.[9] Heil et al.
argue that reporting details alone is insufficient for computational machine-
learning work and require data, models, and code to let third parties obtain the
same results.[10] These sources support recoverability when a computational
result is material. They do not test the Forge template, compare the candidate
with its current semantic gate, or show that one extra checklist line changes
author behavior.

### Burden is conditional and can be large

The candidate is 51 words and names five record classes. The complete bounded-
freshness report grew from 2,407 to 3,204 words, an increase of 797 words or
33.11%. The complete claim-map report grew from 4,718 to 18,850 words, an
increase of 14,132 words or 299.53%. These full-artifact deltas are upper-bound
proxies, not causal package costs: both revisions also corrected substantive
methods and explanations.[3][4]

The corrected packages made their results independently checkable. They also
show why materiality matters. A 22-case executable fixture can be compact enough
to embed, while two unmerged claim-map passes with detailed rows produce much
larger records. Neither full-artifact delta establishes net economic value. The
claim-map evaluation found that its larger record added localization but no
incremental disclosed detection over the current semantic checklist.[4]

### Evidence synthesis

**Known:** two same-model-family readers agree that the current semantic gate and
the candidate classify all eight targets identically; the later evaluations
confirm both historical package defects, both corrected-package controls, and
the independently decidable retained-earnings result.[3][4][5] The candidate is
51 words and names the record classes that external and Brain sources associate
with reproducible computational work.[6][7][9][10]

**Inferred:** enumerating the record classes may make the current obligation more
visible, but no tested classification, disposition, or false-blocker result
shows added decision value. A visibility benefit without changed behavior or
review outcome remains unmeasured.

**Unknown:** prospective author compliance, future defect rate, reviewer time,
different-model or human reader agreement, nonprocedural false-blocker rate, and
whether one sentence could cause authors to narrow immaterial procedural claims
before evaluation rather than merely add records.

## Alternatives and Implications

| Alternative | Checked evidence | Implication |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Keep the current semantic template and skill | Both readers produced the same classifications as the candidate; later evaluations reached the needed corrections and the T5 no-weight boundary.[2][3][4][5] | Simplest supported baseline. It still depends on disciplined semantic reading, and earlier authors have failed to apply it. |
| Add the 51-word conditional template item | It names five record classes and passes the complete and ordinary controls, but changes zero classifications and still blocks T5 as written. | Not supported as adding decision value by this test. |
| Add the item plus one skill sentence | Could emphasize materiality and claim narrowing. | Untested; no evidence here shows changed author behavior or a better T5 classification. |
| Require a complete evidence map | Prior corrected testing produced much larger records and no incremental disclosed detection over the full semantic checklist.[4] | Rejected as disproportionate for this purpose. |
| Build an executable checker | Materiality, claim withdrawal, source sufficiency, and reader-output relevance require semantic judgment; no checked source supplies a deterministic local oracle. | Outside scope and unsupported. |
| Do nothing beyond independent evaluation | Avoids added template text and preserves the current review backstop. | Accepts that package failures may survive until evaluation; prospective incidence and cost are unknown. |

The evidence does not support a proposal for the candidate gate. The root idea's
support threshold required a unique decision-relevant classification, both known
material defects, both corrected controls, no ordinary-control package, and a
claim-narrowing route for the independently decidable retained-earnings case.[1]
The candidate meets every condition except the two decisive ones: it adds no
classification over the current gate and does not route T5 to narrowing as
written. Stopping after this negative research result is therefore the simplest
supported outcome. Evaluation, not this research stage, owns the disposition.

## Response to Feedback and Remaining Questions

No prior evaluation exists for this pipeline.

The root idea's material questions are answered as follows:

1. **Material missing packages:** both gates and readers classify T1 and T3 as
   `package required`, consistent with later REVISE findings.[3][4]
2. **Corrected packages:** both gates and readers classify T2 and T4 as
   `package complete`; no package false blocker appears.[3][4]
3. **Ordinary source research:** both T6 and S2 are `not applicable` under both
   gates and readers.[6]
4. **Synthetic missing package:** S1 is `package required` under both gates and
   readers.
5. **Claim narrowing:** neither gate routes T5 to `claim narrowing sufficient`
   because the report lacks explicit no-weight language; the later evaluator
   supplied that interpretation and decided the result independently.[5]
6. **Incremental decision value:** zero of 16 classifications changes from the
   current baseline to the candidate. The support condition fails.
7. **Proportionality:** the sentence is short, but actual complete-package
   reports show report-size increases from 33.11% to 299.53%; future author and
   reviewer cost remains unmeasured.

No decision-critical evidence gap blocks evaluation of this negative result.
The unmeasured prospective behavior effect cannot rescue the tested claim; it is
an explicit prerequisite for any stronger future hypothesis. A later reopening
would need changed evidence: for example, a prospectively predeclared wording
that produces a unique correct classification or prevents a material package
failure without creating a T5-like blocker against an independently decidable
finding.

Confidence is medium. Confidence is high in the frozen-corpus classification
result because both order-balanced readers agree exactly, the report bodies are
recoverable, and the later evaluations independently confirm the package and
narrowing boundaries.[3][4][5] Overall confidence remains medium because both
readers share one model family, Reader A loaded general skills beyond its target
set, the corpus is retrospective and small, only one independently decidable
narrowing case was tested, and no author-behavior or review-cost outcome was
measured. Confidence would rise if different-model or human readers reproduce
the null result prospectively. It would fall if a concise conditional rule
produced a unique, correct classification over the full current contract without
blocking independently decidable negative research.

This report issues no verdict, proposal, template edit, skill edit,
implementation, or learning change. It returns the completed negative evidence
to independent evaluation.
