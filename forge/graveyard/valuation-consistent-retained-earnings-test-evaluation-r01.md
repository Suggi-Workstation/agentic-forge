## Findings

### The existing rule already contains the decisive semantic safeguards

The current management framework does not define a pass as a favorable share-
price ratio. It requires each retained dollar to create owner value through
incremental returns at or above alternatives, directs the analyst to use actual
project or acquisition outcomes when a denominator is distorted, requires
actual-share reconciliation, and says to score decisions rather than share-
price performance alone. Its score and override rules already keep unresolved
conclusion-critical evidence visible.[3] Berkshire's original rule asks whether
retention produces at least one dollar of market value per dollar retained over
a rolling five-year period while naming per-share intrinsic-value growth as the
objective. Brain prior work accordingly treats the market test as a rough
retrospective proxy that must be supplemented by incremental-return and per-
share evidence.[14][15]

The company template separately requires capital-deployment, actual-share,
limitation, confidence, scorecard, and framework-application evidence. It does
not require a three-label display, but it does not authorize a market-only
capital-allocation pass either.[3] The candidate's separate labels make the
existing semantics harder to compress incorrectly. That is a presentational
benefit, not evidence that the present framework reaches a different supported
decision.

### Nintendo's arithmetic reproduces, including the denominator problem

Primary Nintendo records and independent fixed arithmetic reproduce the
research's aligned March 2021-to-March 2026 bridge:[5][6][7][8][9]

- JPY4,032.60 earnings per share times 15.33 PER, divided by the later 10-for-1
  split, gives JPY6,181.9758 per opening share. Issued shares less treasury
  shares, on the same split basis, equal 1,191,227,670. Their product is
  JPY7,364,140.628 million of opening market capitalization.
- JPY364.51 earnings per share times 24.07 PER gives JPY8,773.7557 per closing
  share. Issued shares less treasury shares equal 1,152,828,705. Their product
  is JPY10,114,637.422 million, a JPY2,750,496.793 million increase.
- FY2022-FY2026 profit attributable to owners sums to JPY2,103,923 million;
  dividends sum to JPY1,056,933 million; and gross treasury-share purchases sum
  to JPY245,756 million. Retention after dividends is therefore JPY1,046,990
  million, and net retention after dividends and repurchases is JPY801,234
  million. The aligned market ratios are 2.627 and 3.433, respectively.

The issuer tables also confirm the FY2022 JPY31,607 million retained-earnings
cancellation reclassification and the FY2026 JPY28,795 million transfer from
retained earnings to capital surplus. Neither is an additional owner
payment.[6][9] Native PDF text was checked against rendered table pages; the field,
unit, period, and column associations agreed despite nonmaterial Poppler font-
mapping warnings.

The published Nintendo report's approximately 2.2 ratio is also conditionally
reproducible. Its JPY7,896 report-date price and post-July actual-share count,
which reflects a 44,500-share restricted-stock disposal, produce a
JPY1,738,945.496 million gain from the March 2021 opening point. That is 2.170
against net-of-buybacks retention but 1.661 against retention after dividends
only.[4][5][9][10] The report did not state the denominator and mixed a September 2026
market endpoint with March 2026 earnings and distributions. The research is
therefore correct to call the 2.2 figure a rough, convention-dependent cross-
check rather than an unqualified retained-earnings result.[1]

The aligned market cross-check is favorable under either stated denominator.
That does not establish that retained capital produced intrinsic value. The
current Nintendo report has no contemporaneous March 2021 intrinsic-value
estimate, and the operating comparison spans a console cycle for which no clean
corresponding incremental-capital denominator was established.[1][4] Under both
the candidate and the full current rule, market evidence is supported while
operating and historical intrinsic-value evidence remain indeterminate. Neither
rule supports an overall retained-earnings pass.

### Crocs confirms that the current rule can use decision-specific evidence

Crocs paid USD2.05 billion cash and issued 2,852,280 shares for HEYDUDE, with
USD2.0 billion of term debt and USD50 million of revolver financing. Aggregate
consideration was approximately USD2.3 billion.[11] HEYDUDE then produced
USD137.401 million of 2024 segment operating income but USD714.840 million of
2025 revenue, a USD668.855 million segment operating loss, and USD737 million of
trademark and goodwill impairments after lower growth and margin expectations
and higher discount rates.[12][13]

This is adverse project-specific evidence under the current framework's full-
cost and actual-outcome test. A forced five-year market bridge or hindsight
intrinsic-value endpoint is unnecessary and unavailable. The Crocs report's 2/5
capital-allocation score, Medium confidence, and no-override result remain the
same under both rules.[1][4] The control therefore supports enforcing the
current rule, not adding a mandatory market or intrinsic-value field.

### The comparison is decision-complete despite one procedural limitation

The target states that two isolated, order-balanced readers used the same model
family and that their material calculations, labels, and disagreements were
reproduced in the report. Their distinct raw outputs are not linked or preserved
as separate records.[1] The Forge's existing evidence-preservation lesson says
unmerged reader outputs are required before a multi-reader procedural comparison
is treated as independently checkable.[15] Reader agreement therefore receives
no additional evidentiary weight in this evaluation.

That limitation does not require another research cycle. The evaluator directly
checked the exact current-rule text, company reports, primary issuer and SEC
records, rendered Nintendo tables, and arithmetic. Those checks reproduce the
negative decision comparison without relying on the unavailable reader records:

- Nintendo remains 3/5, Medium confidence, no override, and no supported overall
  retained-earnings pass under both rules;
- Crocs remains 2/5, Medium confidence, no override, and no supported overall
  retained-earnings pass under both rules;
- neither report changes a refusal result; and
- the only supported change is to correct Nintendo's denominator, endpoint, and
  blended wording in that report.[1][3][4]

The strongest support for the candidate is error prevention: explicit labels
could make a future market-only misapplication less likely. The contrary and
decisive evidence is that the present framework already forbids the substantive
error, the template already requires the relevant evidence, and this bounded
test changed zero decisions. A larger or prospective sample might establish a
recurring enforcement defect, but this report does not.[1][3]

## Verdict and Handoff

**Verdict: REJECT. Next stage: closed in `forge/graveyard/`. Zero of two
corrective cycles were used; this closure consumes no corrective cycle.**

The exact research target is grounded enough to decide the bounded question,
and its negative result matches independent checks. A report-only correction is
the smallest supported response to Nintendo's ambiguous market-only wording.
The candidate framework sentence and template field add no demonstrated score,
confidence, override, refusal, or capital-allocation classification change
beyond correct application of the current rule. The root idea explicitly names
no decision change, stylistic output only, and current-rule sufficiency as stop
conditions.[1][2]

A revision would ask for evidence whose absence does not block the negative
result. A reframe would need a different empirical question, such as repeated
misapplication across a larger corpus, rather than another wording variant.
Reopening requires new independent evidence or a prospective, predeclared test
showing that separate labels prevent recurring market-only errors despite full
enforcement of the current rule and produce a decision-relevant benefit or a
lower total review cost. This verdict does not authorize a Nintendo report edit,
framework change, template change, or implementation.[16]

Confidence is medium. Confidence is high in the exact current-rule comparison,
Nintendo source amounts and arithmetic, Crocs acquisition and impairment facts,
and the zero decision delta for the two tested reports.[3][5][9][11][12][13] Overall
confidence remains medium because the corpus has two reports, only one explicit
market test, no prospective control, no historical intrinsic-value series, and
no preserved separate-reader outputs. Confidence would rise if an independent
larger or prospective test reproduced the zero delta, and would fall if exact
current-rule application changed a tested score or if separate labels uniquely
prevented a documented decision error.

## Learning Decision

`LEARNINGS.md` gains one medium-confidence process lesson: prefer bounded native
file/search tools and fixed direct commands over inline interpreter or heredoc
probes in unattended Forge stages.[15][16]

- **Selection:** The root idea's explicit stop condition and comparison with the
  full current rule made the zero decision delta decisive. No new selection
  lesson is needed.
- **Evidence and test design:** Exact framework text, primary statements, table
  render checks, and fixed arithmetic decided the question. Missing separate-
  reader records repeat the existing high-confidence preservation lesson, so
  that lesson is not duplicated or raised.
- **Process:** This evaluation repeated a cross-pipeline pattern: an inline
  Python heredoc used only to read log tails was blocked before execution, while
  `read_file`, `search_files`, and fixed commands supplied the same evidence.
  Four independent pipelines now record this failure class and successful
  bounded alternatives in `logbook/errors.log`.[16]
- **Repetition:** The blocked-probe pattern recurs across independent pipelines,
  agents, and stages; leaving it only in chronology has not prevented reuse.
- **Coverage:** The new lesson governs tool choice inside unattended Forge work,
  carries no runtime or governance permission, and remains below High because no
  prospective stage has yet shown that applying it eliminates the failure.
