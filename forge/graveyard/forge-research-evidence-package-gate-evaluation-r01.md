# Evaluation: Conditional Evidence-Package Gate

## Target and Baseline

Target: `forge/research/forge-research-evidence-package-gate-r01.md`, ID
`20261001T020825Z`.[1]

The target author is Researcher. This evaluation was performed by Analyst in a
separate scheduled context that did not inherit the research drafting session's
private reasoning. Analyst authored the root idea, but the protocol permits an
ideator to evaluate another agent's research after recording a cold baseline.
The exact research target therefore passes the independent-author and context
gate.[2][8]

At starting HEAD `ae5175d5f89145a565304e6f6c5f4fd1d3b4b0b0`, the worktree was
clean. The board contained one valid unselected discovery awaiting human review
and one active `evaluate` row for this exact target. Progress ended at ENT-027
and errors at ENT-033. The selected pipeline has no prior `REVISE` or `REFRAME`,
so zero of two corrective cycles were used.[8]

Before reading the target body, the expected evidence and failure conditions
were recorded in evaluator scratch. Expected evidence was:

- the current research template and skill applied as their full semantic
  contract, not as an item-presence baseline;
- one frozen candidate rule and four explicit outcomes applied to both material
  missing-package reports, both corrected controls, the independently decidable
  retained-earnings report, an ordinary source-synthesis control, and both
  synthetic controls;
- separate cold-reader classifications and reasons, including order,
  independence limits, contamination, disagreements, and the later-verdict
  boundary;
- a recoverable procedural record sufficient to check material classifications,
  counts, and reader-agreement claims;
- a unique decision-relevant classification over the full current baseline,
  both historical defects caught, both corrected packages accepted, no ordinary
  false blocker, and a valid claim-narrowing route for the independently
  decidable result; and
- proportionate burden, alternatives, uncertainty, and no claim of measured
  future author behavior.

Failure conditions included an artificially weak current baseline, equal
classifications represented as incremental value, an omitted or misclassified
control, unavailable material reader records, answer-key leakage, unsupported
future-effect claims, or a recommendation broader than one reversible template
item and at most one procedure sentence.

## Findings

### The full current contract already detects every tested package boundary

The current template requires the investigated method and checks actually
performed, important source passages or results, limitations, reproducible
source checks, independent support or explicit unknowns, and no invented tests.
The research skill additionally requires checked evidence, contrary evidence,
alternatives, and explicit uncertainty.[2] This is a semantic evidence contract,
not a section-presence test.

The target freezes that full baseline before the candidate. Its 51-word
candidate enumerates five record classes and adds an explicit instruction to
withdraw or give no weight to an unsupported procedural claim when the finding
is independently decidable.[1] Independent reading of the complete target
corpus reproduces these paired classifications:

| Case | Full current contract | Candidate | Checked consequence |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| T1 initial bounded-freshness report | `package required` | `package required` | Material replay bytes, fixtures, and raw outputs were unavailable; the later evaluation returned REVISE.[3] |
| T2 corrected bounded-freshness report | `package complete` | `package complete` | Exact classifier, fixtures, invocation, and raw results are embedded; the later evaluation independently reran 22/22 cases.[3] |
| T3 initial claim-map report | `package required` | `package required` | Exact instrument, unmerged reader output, and a full semantic comparator record were unavailable; the later evaluation returned REVISE.[4] |
| T4 corrected claim-map report | `package complete` | `package complete` | Exact instrument and distinct method/reader records are embedded; the later evaluation verified the package and rejected the method on decision value.[4] |
| T5 retained-earnings report as written | `package required` | `package required` | Separate reader records are unavailable and the report does not expressly withdraw or give no weight to the reader comparison.[5] |
| T6 ordinary source synthesis | `not applicable` | `not applicable` | It synthesizes published evidence and reports no new local procedural comparison.[6] |

The evaluator therefore reproduces zero classification changes across the eight
cases. This agrees with both reported reader passes and independently confirms
the decision-relevant null without relying on their agreement claim.[1] The
candidate makes the record classes easier to quote, but the tested current
contract reaches the same package, completion, and not-applicable boundaries.
That fails the root idea's predeclared requirement for a unique
classification.[2]

### T5 does not rescue the candidate

T5 is independently decidable: its later evaluator reproduced the rule,
primary records, and arithmetic, gave unavailable reader agreement no
additional weight, and closed the pipeline without another research cycle.[5]
The T5 research artifact itself does not contain that explicit no-weight
statement. Both gates therefore correctly classify the artifact as written as
`package required`; neither may infer a withdrawal that the artifact did not
make.[1][5]

The candidate could prompt a future author to add one no-weight sentence before
publication. That is a plausible behavior effect, not a tested classification
or decision effect. The experiment did not expose an author to the candidate,
measure whether the sentence was added, or compare review cost after the edit.
The missing prospective effect cannot be converted into support for a proposal.
A future test may reopen that narrower hypothesis, but it does not reverse this
pipeline's failed support condition.[1][2]

### Reader agreement is not independently checkable, but the negative result is

The target preserves separate summary tables of Reader A and Reader B
classifications and concise reasons. It does not embed or link their complete
unmerged outputs, exact prompt or invocation records, or raw synthetic-control
responses. Reader A also loaded two general Forge skill files outside the
intended target-only set.[1][8] The claim of exact reader agreement therefore
receives no additional evidentiary weight under the existing package-
preservation lesson.[8]

This procedural limitation does not require a correction cycle. The evaluator
read the exact baseline, candidate, six real targets, later evaluations, and
both synthetic descriptions and reproduced the same eight paired
classifications. As in T5, an unsupported reader-agreement claim can receive no
weight when direct checks independently decide the bounded question.[1][3][4][5]
The report's negative result is therefore decision-complete even though its
reader-agreement evidence is not a complete package.

### Counts, burden, and external evidence are represented without inflation

Fixed `wc -w` checks reproduced 2,407 and 3,204 words for the bounded-freshness
reports and 4,718 and 18,850 words for the claim-map reports. The corresponding
increases are 797 words and 14,132 words, matching the target's 33.11% and
299.53% calculations.[3][4] These whole-artifact differences include method and
explanation corrections and are correctly labeled upper-bound proxies rather
than causal package costs.[1]

Brain sources independently state that an evaluation contract includes the
system, environment, protocol, grader, and raw run artifacts, and that a trace
supports only the operations and fields it records.[6] ACM identifies data and
software systems as artifacts that can add value beyond the article text, while
Heil et al. recommend data, model, and code publication plus automation for
computational reproducibility.[7] These sources support recoverable evidence.
They do not compare the local candidate with the current Forge contract or show
that one extra checklist line changes behavior.[1][7]

The target also compares doing nothing, the candidate item, one additional
skill sentence, a large evidence map, and an executable checker. It preserves
the retrospective sample, same-model-family readers, Reader A deviation,
unmeasured author behavior, unknown review cost, and semantic-judgment boundary.
No external source or local result converts those unknowns into an affirmative
benefit.[1]

## Verdict and Handoff

**Verdict: REJECT. Graveyard closure; there is no next stage. Zero of two
corrective cycles were used, and this terminal verdict consumes no corrective
cycle.**

The research produces a decisive negative result. The full current research
contract and the candidate classify all eight tested cases identically. Both
catch the two material historical package defects, pass both corrected
packages, avoid both ordinary-source false blockers, and require explicit
narrowing before treating T5 as narrowed.[1][3][4][5] The candidate therefore
adds wording specificity but no tested decision-relevant classification, while
its prospective author-behavior and review-cost effects remain unknown. The
root idea explicitly names equal performance by the fair current gate as a stop
condition.[2]

The selected pipeline closes. Remove only pipeline `20261001T013556Z` from
`STATUS.md`; preserve the unrelated human-review row. No proposal, template
change, skill change, learning change, implementation, or approval is
authorized.[8]

Reopening requires changed evidence: a prospectively predeclared candidate must
cause a unique correct package or claim-narrowing action over the full current
contract, prevent a material preservation defect or unnecessary correction, and
show decision value or lower total review cost without false blockers. Renaming
the same enumeration or repeating the retrospective cases is not changed
evidence.

Confidence in REJECT is high for the tested classification hypothesis because
the exact current contract, candidate, six real artifacts, later evaluations,
and independent evaluator classifications agree.[1][2][3][4][5] Overall
artifact confidence remains medium because the corpus is small and
retrospective, the reported readers share one model family, their complete raw
records are unavailable, Reader A exceeded its reading boundary, and no author-
behavior or review-cost outcome was measured. Confidence would fall if a
prospective controlled application produced a unique correct action over the
full current contract without a T5-like false blocker.

## Learning Decision

`LEARNINGS.md` remains unchanged.[8]

- **Selection:** The root idea's full-contract comparator and explicit stop
  condition made the zero classification change decisive. No new selection
  rule is needed.
- **Evidence and test design:** Exact corpus reads and a fair semantic baseline
  made the negative result reproducible. The missing complete reader records
  mean agreement receives no added weight, but direct evaluator classification
  independently decides the result. The existing package-preservation lesson
  already states this method.
- **Process:** One unattended `execute_code` attempt to parse a spill file was
  blocked before execution. Paginated `read_file` calls plus fixed `fold` and
  `wc` commands recovered the evidence without a repository or runtime change.
  The existing unattended-tooling lesson already covers this route choice.
- **Repetition:** The target again omits complete unmerged reader records, but
  the current high-confidence lesson already names that exact failure. This is
  nonapplication, not a wording gap, and the pipeline does not supply a later
  prospective success that would justify another confidence change.
- **Coverage:** A new lesson would duplicate the package-preservation,
  full-contract-comparator, and unattended-tooling entries. No learning edit
  passes the separate admission gate.
