# Proposal: One Prospective Company-Report Cold-Review Trial

## Proposed Decision

Approve, for separate implementation and review, one reversible prospective
trial on the first eligible unseen Investing Hub company-report draft. The trial
compares the report author's current full-contract self-review with one separate
cold full-contract review of the same frozen draft and evidence packet. It then
uses a third, separately recorded adjudication to measure which material defects
were caught, missed, or falsely raised. This is a controlled process trial, not
a permanent release gate, analytical-rule change, report field, claim map,
company-report edit, security recommendation, or investment recommendation.[1][3]

The problem is narrow. Three earlier Forge pipelines found material application
defects in the two current company reports even though the existing management,
DCF, financial-health, and company-template rules already required the supported
corrections. The corrected retrospective experiment then showed that two strict
target-only readers HALTed all six challenged originals and PASSed all six
corrected controls. Exact inputs, outputs, observable access receipts, and
independent evaluation support that capability result. They do not show
incremental performance on an unseen report, prospective error reduction, packet
assembly without hindsight, or total workflow cost.[1][2][3]

In plain terms, another checklist is not the smallest useful change. The smallest
useful change is to take one naturally arising draft before release, freeze what
the author and a cold reviewer can actually inspect, let both apply the rules
that already exist, and measure whether the cold pass changes a supported
release decision enough to justify its burden. The first review must be blind to
Forge verdicts, expected defects, and the author's review output. The trial must
preserve a valid negative or uncertain finding rather than reward only positive
conclusions.[3][5]

The beneficiary is Suggi's company-research process: the trial can show whether
scarce review attention catches material unsupported stewardship, funding, or
valuation language before publication, or whether it merely repeats the author,
adds false blockers, or reconstructs the research at disproportionate cost. The
exact requested decision is approval to run this one trial and return its
measured result for a later decision. Even a successful trial does not approve a
permanent review requirement.[3][6]

The trial's non-goals are:

- changing `investing-hub:governance/template-company.md` or any analytical
  framework;
- editing an existing company report through this proposal;
- creating a mandatory report field, retained claim inventory, generic evidence
  package, executable checker, service, cron job, runtime component, or profile
  rule;
- using the retrospective Nintendo or Crocs passages as the prospective target;
- treating reader agreement as factual corroboration or hidden-reasoning proof;
- measuring investment performance, recommending a security, or authorizing a
  portfolio action; and
- inferring a permanent gate, fleet-wide benefit, or causal error-rate reduction
  from one report.[2][3][4]

## Evidence, Alternatives, and Evaluation Response

### Why one trial is the smallest supported step

The root idea predeclared that a positive retrospective result could support at
most a bounded prospective trial. Its stopping conditions included a missed
material DCF or liquidity defect, a false HALT on a corrected control, answer-key
dependence, unsupported scope expansion, and evidence burden comparable to the
underlying research.[1] The first research revision reproduced all twelve
original/control classifications, but its answer-key-blind execution boundary
was not inspectable. The first evaluation therefore returned REVISE rather than
advancing a plausible result over missing procedural evidence.[2]

The corrected research did not reconstruct unavailable history. It ran a new
pair, embedded exact packet and output bytes, preserved complete observable
receipts, and retained the retrospective, curated, same-model-family, and cost
limits. Both readers again HALTed every original and PASSed every corrected
control. The exact evaluation independently reproduced the byte identities,
classifications, current-rule applications, and material calculations and then
returned ADVANCE only for a reversible prospective trial.[2][3]

The evaluation also corrected the burden interpretation. The 69- and 65-second
intervals ended before JSON construction, output writing, and final return; the
complete delegated task runtimes were 246.09 and 240.48 seconds. Packet assembly,
author work, reviewer handling, revision, and final disposition were not
measured. The proposed trial therefore records complete end-to-end and active
burden by role instead of reusing the shorter intervals as total review cost.[3]

The current company template already requires every applicable item to be
verified against the actual report, sources, and calculation records before
release. Its management, financial-health, and valuation contracts already
require actual-share and incremental-return support, dated stressed funding,
claim-matched valuation methods, N/A for unsupported values, and explicit
uncertainty. A supported negative or limited assessment may PASS; unsupported
claims and misleading completion labels HALT.[5] The trial holds these semantics
fixed so that a role/context comparison is not confused with a new analytical
rule.

Brain prior work supplies design constraints, not a local effect estimate. A
review packet should expose the exact target, evidence, uncertainty, effect,
alternatives, and checks; fresh review and self-review are different evidence
classes; same-model agreement is not independent assurance; and outcome metrics
must distinguish helpful detection from false escalation and reviewer burden.[6]
CFA Institute Standard V(A) independently requires diligence, independence,
thoroughness, an adequate research basis, measurable research-quality criteria,
and written scenario-testing guidance. It supports testing a disciplined review
process but does not establish that this specific cold-review design will add
value.[7]

### Provisional explanation, gaps, and disconfirmation

The provisional explanation is that an author can remain anchored to the model,
source choices, and conclusions used to draft a report, while a cold reader may
apply the same contracts to the frozen evidence without that production context.
The retrospective pair shows this is possible on curated passages. It does not
show that a normal report packet contains the evidence needed for an unseen
review or that the added reader improves the final decision.[2][3]

The main alternative explanations remain live:

- the retrospective result may depend on hindsight-selected passages and a
  packet built from already adjudicated defects;
- a cold reader may repeat the same model-family error, reject a supported
  negative finding, or expand beyond the report's evidence;
- the author may catch the same defects during the existing hard gate, leaving
  no incremental detection;
- packet preparation and adjudication may cost as much as reconstructing the
  underlying research; and
- a report with no material defect may reveal burden and false-HALT risk but
  cannot estimate miss detection.[3][6]

The design is disconfirmed as a candidate permanent control if the cold reviewer
misses a decision-changing adjudicated defect, falsely HALTs a materially
supported passage, adds a material unsupported finding, accesses an answer key,
or requires new source discovery or calculation reconstruction to perform the
review. It also fails to show incremental value if its supported findings are a
subset of the author's frozen self-review or do not change wording, score,
classification, valuation status, thesis, confidence, or release disposition.
One clean trial without a defect remains informative about feasibility and cost,
but not about detection benefit.

### Alternatives

| Alternative | Evidence and benefit | Disposition |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Keep only the current author hard gate | It is the simplest current process and already contains the governing semantics.[5] | Retain as the control and as the default after trial reversal; actual prospective performance is unknown. |
| Correct known reports only | Earlier domain work identifies bounded report corrections. | May repair known text if separately authorized, but it does not test prevention and is outside this proposal. |
| Run the proposed one-report trial | Directly measures author/cold-review overlap, unique findings, false HALTs, packet feasibility, full burden, revisions, and decision effect on unseen work. | Recommended as the smallest reversible information-producing change. |
| Add a permanent cold-review gate now | Could prevent some unsupported releases. | Rejected: one curated retrospective pair does not establish prospective benefit or proportional cost.[3] |
| Add a field, bridge, rule, or claim map | Could make selected checks more visible. | Rejected for this purpose; the tested defects were already governed by current rules, and prior added structures showed no unique decision value.[1][3] |
| Use post-publication sampled audit | Avoids blocking release and measures residual defects. | Useful future comparator, but it cannot prevent the first publication error and has not been tested here. |
| Do nothing | Adds no burden. | Leaves the observed recurrence and incremental-review question unresolved. |

### Response to every ADVANCE finding

1. **Prospective and predeclared:** the first eligible draft and trial rules are
   selected before either review result exists. Retrospective CROX/NTDOY units
   and Forge verdicts are excluded from the review packet.[3]
2. **Unseen report revision:** both author and cold reviewer inspect the same
   exact draft bytes before either sees the other's output. The cold reviewer did
   not author the report and receives no private author reasoning.
3. **Complete semantic comparator:** both reviews apply the exact current company
   template and every applicable current framework. A presence checklist or the
   rejected claim map is not a comparator.[3][5]
4. **Observable receipts:** exact target, contract, source/calculation, prompt,
   role, tool-access, output, timing, and revision identities are retained. The
   receipt claims only observable access, not hidden reasoning.[2][3]
5. **Misses and false HALTs:** a third frozen adjudication classifies material
   report defects and each author/cold-review finding under the same contracts.
   It records caught-by-author, caught-by-cold-reviewer, caught-by-both,
   caught-by-neither, false HALT, unresolved, and unsupported expansion.
6. **Full burden:** packet assembly, author review, complete cold-review task,
   adjudication, revision, and final verification each receive active and elapsed
   time plus byte/word and tool-call counts. Short internal analysis timestamps
   are not reported as total task duration.[3]
7. **No permanence or implementation claim:** the trial returns evidence for a
   later decision. It changes no template, framework, standing workflow, report,
   runtime, or deployment through this Forge proposal.[4]

No new decision-critical evidence is needed to specify this one-trial design.
Whether the cold review adds value is the trial's measured question, not a fact
assumed by the proposal.

## Change Specification and Implementation

### Existing interfaces and proposed trial records

The existing analytical interfaces remain unchanged:

- `investing-hub:governance/template-company.md` supplies the current release
  hard gate and report contract;
- the applicable files under `investing-hub:frameworks/` supply the analytical
  rules; and
- one separately authorized file under `investing-hub:companies/` supplies the
  eligible draft.[5]

The proposed implementation creates only task-owned trial records outside the
production repositories until a separately authorized normal report revision is
ready to be written. These names describe proposed records; they do not exist
because of this proposal:

1. `trial-manifest.json` - frozen eligibility, selection, roles, model/runtime,
   source cutoff, contract revisions, outcome rubric, stop rules, and authorized
   paths;
2. `draft-v0.md` - exact author-declared review-ready report bytes before either
   review, with path, Git revision, byte count, and SHA-256;
3. `evidence-pack.md` - the exact report, complete applicable contract bytes,
   and source/calculation records already available at the review-ready point;
4. `author-review.json` - the author's ordinary full hard-gate result on v0,
   including claim-level findings, proposed changes, uncertainty, and burden;
5. `cold-review.json` - the separate reader's result on the same v0 and packet,
   using the same output contract and no author-review or Forge access;
6. `adjudication.json` - a third context's contract-based disposition of the
   report defects and both review outputs after they are frozen;
7. `revision-receipt.json` - exact authorized v0-to-final report changes, review
   rounds, verification, and final release disposition; and
8. `burden.json` - packet, author, cold-reader, adjudicator, revision, and total
   active/elapsed time, bytes, words, tool calls, failures, and exclusions.

The scratch paths, retention, and cleanup method are chosen by the separately
authorized implementer. Exact bytes needed to verify the result must be retained
in the final trial evidence package; a hash without available bytes is not a
recoverable record.[2][4]

### Eligibility and predeclaration

The manifest selects the first report that meets all of these conditions after
trial authorization:

- it is a new full company report or a material analytical revision, not a
  formatting-only edit;
- it reaches an author-declared review-ready draft under the current company
  template;
- it is not a replay or correction of the six retrospective Nintendo/Crocs units;
- its author, cold reviewer, and adjudicator are identified before outputs are
  shared, and the cold reviewer did not author the draft; and
- the exact report sources and calculation records used by the author can be
  frozen without new hindsight-driven source discovery.

If the first candidate cannot satisfy the evidence boundary, the trial records
`Inconclusive` with an infeasible-evidence-boundary reason and stops. It does not
silently choose an easier report. No report is required to contain a planted
defect, and no defect may be inserted for the trial.

### Ordered implementation

A separately authorized implementer should execute this sequence:

1. Freeze `trial-manifest.json` before either review begins. Record the exact
   current template and applicable framework commits and hashes, role identities,
   model families, permissions, output schemas, stop rules, burden fields, and
   first-eligible selection rule.
2. At the report author's ordinary review-ready point, freeze v0 and the exact
   evidence already used to draft it. Build the neutral packet mechanically from
   v0, the full current contracts, cited source passages or retained source bytes,
   and calculation records. Do not add a defect label, Forge artifact, expected
   conclusion, or evidence found after seeing a review result.
3. Start the author's current full-contract self-review and the cold review from
   the same v0. Keep their outputs mutually hidden until both are complete. The
   author may use the normal drafting context; the cold reviewer receives only
   the frozen packet and authorized read-only tools. Preserve complete end-to-end
   receipts for both.
4. Require each review to classify every applicable hard-gate item and every
   material challenge. For each challenge record exact report text, governing
   rule, evidence, PASS/HALT, requested change, decision effect, uncertainty, and
   any scope expansion. A supported negative, Limited, Not calculable, Unknown,
   Unclear, Fragile, or other adverse result may PASS.
5. Freeze both outputs, then run adjudication in a third context that did not
   author v0 or either review. The adjudicator receives v0, the frozen packet,
   both outputs, and the same contracts. It must inspect the whole target, not
   only disputed rows, and classify material defects plus false or unsupported
   findings. Any decision-critical item that requires new source discovery is
   `unresolved`; it is not guessed.
6. Produce the comparison ledger: adjudicated material defects caught by author,
   cold reviewer, both, or neither; false HALTs; unsupported expansions; unique
   supported cold-review findings; and exact effects on report wording, score,
   class, confidence, valuation status, thesis, or release disposition.
7. If separately authorized under the normal Investing Hub workflow, revise the
   report only for adjudicated supported findings. Preserve v0 and every revision.
   Permit one correction pass and one final verification pass. A remaining
   material unsupported claim stops the trial and leaves release disposition
   blocked or unresolved under the ordinary report process; the trial does not
   waive that process.
8. Record complete burden. Packet assembly starts with candidate selection and
   ends with the frozen packet. Reader burden starts with dispatch and ends after
   durable output write and return. Record author review, adjudication, revision,
   verification, waiting, failures, and human handling separately. Do not combine
   parallel intervals by simple addition without labeling them.
9. Return the exact trial package for independent review. Stop. Do not amend the
   template, create a standing gate, schedule another sample, or deploy a workflow
   automatically.

The report author remains responsible for the normal hard gate. The cold pass is
a treatment, not a substitute. The adjudicator resolves only this trial's
measurement record and does not receive standing authority over Investing Hub.
Any report write, publication, later process amendment, or second trial requires
its own authorization.

### Outcome contract

The trial reports evidence, not a forced PASS. It must return one of four exact
outcome classes:

- **Incremental signal:** the cold reviewer uniquely finds at least one
  adjudicated material issue that changes a report decision field or release
  disposition, with no decision-changing miss, material false HALT, answer-key
  exposure, or material unsupported expansion.
- **No incremental signal:** the cold review adds no unique adjudicated material
  finding over the author's complete review, regardless of agreement.
- **Harmful signal:** the cold reviewer misses an adjudicated decision-changing
  issue, materially false-HALTs supported text, or adds a material unsupported
  finding.
- **Inconclusive:** contamination, missing exact bytes, unresolved role or target
  identity, unavailable evidence, hash mismatch, unadjudicable material claim,
  incomplete receipt, or interrupted run prevents the comparison.

Burden and decision effect are reported beside every class. `Incremental signal`
from one case is not equivalent to approval of a permanent gate. `No incremental
signal`, `Harmful signal`, or `Inconclusive` must not be reframed as success.

## Acceptance, Risks, and Reversal

### Acceptance and regression checks

Before the separately authorized trial starts, require:

- the manifest predates both review outputs and names the first eligible candidate;
- v0, contract, packet, prompt, schema, and role identities have exact bytes,
  revisions, counts, and hashes;
- the author and cold reviewer receive the same v0 and analytical contract;
- the cold packet contains no Forge artifact, retrospective expected label,
  adjudication, author-review output, or other answer key;
- no evidence is added after either review result to make that result appear
  foreseeable; and
- author, cold-reviewer, and adjudicator writes are isolated to explicit paths.

PASS for a complete trial record requires all of the following:

1. Exact input/output bytes and observable access receipts reconstruct every role.
2. The cold reviewer used the full current semantic contract, not item presence.
3. The adjudicator inspected the full v0 target and all material findings under
   the same frozen contract.
4. Every adjudicated defect and review challenge appears exactly once in the
   comparison ledger with source dependence and uncertainty preserved.
5. Miss, false-HALT, unsupported-expansion, unique-finding, revision, and final-
   decision counts recompute from the underlying records.
6. Packet assembly, full author review, complete cold-reader task, adjudication,
   revision, verification, and total burden are separately measurable.
7. A valid negative or uncertain company finding is never counted as a defect
   merely because it is adverse.
8. No report, template, framework, runtime, or standing workflow changes without
   separate authorization and exact-target review.

Negative and regression fixtures must verify that the trial becomes
`Inconclusive` rather than continuing after a missing source record, changed v0,
wrong contract revision, answer-key exposure, extra unauthorized read, missing
receipt, hash mismatch, role collision, or incomplete adjudication. The grader
must also distinguish a supported HALT from a false HALT and a unique finding
from a reworded duplicate. A zero-defect report is a valid case: it may measure
burden and false escalation but cannot be reported as evidence of defect-capture
benefit.

### Worst failure and prevention

The worst plausible failure is false assurance: a second reader appears to have
independently cleared a report while sharing the same hidden premise, seeing an
answer key, missing a decision-changing defect, or reviewing an evidence packet
curated after the outcome. That could make unsupported value, solvency, or
stewardship language look more credible than an ordinary single review.
Prevention is prospective first-candidate selection, one frozen v0, neutral
pre-outcome packet construction, mutually hidden author and reviewer outputs,
complete observable receipts, full-target third adjudication, exact identity
checks, and an outcome class that treats misses or contamination as harmful or
inconclusive rather than PASS.[3][6]

The opposite failure is excessive blocking: the cold reviewer may reject a
supported adverse or uncertain result, invent work outside the evidence, or
create a review burden approaching a second research project. The trial records
false HALTs and unsupported expansion explicitly. It returns `Inconclusive` when
material adjudication requires new source discovery or reconstruction absent
from the review-ready records. It permits only one candidate, one correction
pass, and one final verification pass.

Costs include packet preparation, three recorded judgments, duplicated reading,
source-byte retention, review latency, and possible same-model-family
correlation. One case cannot estimate a stable miss rate, false-HALT rate, or
benefit distribution. Human handling time and any report delay must be reported,
not hidden behind model wall-clock measurements.[3][6]

### Reversal and residual uncertainty

Reversal is direct: stop the trial, remove only uncommitted task-owned scratch
orchestration, and return the company-report process to its unchanged author hard
gate. No template, framework, service, repository schema, historical report,
cron entry, runtime, or deployed gate needs migration. Preserve any completed
trial evidence needed to explain the decision; do not erase a completed negative
result. A separately authorized factual report correction is not reversed merely
to restore the pretrial error.

Residual uncertainty remains material. One unseen report may contain no defect,
may not represent other sectors, and may use the same model family across roles.
Adjudication is itself fallible. Packet availability may depend on author habits,
and the trial does not estimate long-run reviewer learning, queueing, or
maintenance cost. These limits require a later human decision even after a clean
trial; no outcome automatically installs or repeats the control.

Confidence in this proposal is medium. Confidence is high that the design answers
the evaluation's specified measurement obligations because it freezes one target,
uses the complete current contracts, preserves exact role receipts, and records
misses, false HALTs, unsupported expansion, full burden, revisions, and decision
effect.[3][5] Overall confidence remains medium because the prospective workflow
has not run, packet assembly without hindsight is untested, same-model
correlation remains possible, and one case cannot establish a permanent policy.
Confidence would rise if the exact trial completes with a reconstructable record,
low burden, and an adjudicated unique decision-relevant detection. It would fall
with contamination, an unadjudicable packet, a decision-changing miss, a material
false HALT, or burden comparable to reconstructing the report research.

Implementation and deployment remain separately authorized work. This proposal
changes no Investing Hub file and grants no release, security, or portfolio
authority.[4]
