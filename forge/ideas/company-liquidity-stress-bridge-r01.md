# Company Liquidity Stress Bridge

## Question and Value

Can an independent reader reproduce the stressed-liquidity headroom figures in
the two current Investing Hub company reports, and would one compact dated cash
bridge change a financial-health classification, confidence, final thesis, or
first-shortfall finding beyond explicit enforcement of the current financial-
health contract? The bounded targets are the current Crocs and Nintendo report
applications and, only if a repeated output gap is demonstrated,
`investing-hub:governance/template-company.md` or
`investing-hub:frameworks/financial-health.md`.[1][2][3][4]

The current framework requires a dated liquidity bridge from opening usable cash,
stressed cash generation, accessible facilities, debt maturities, and other
mandatory uses; it also requires the first shortfall or credible headroom to
remain visible. The company template requires the decisive result in the report
but permits detailed workings to remain temporary and does not require a retained
companion calculation.[2] Crocs reports two-year stress headroom of USD69 million
above a USD100 million cash floor and names stressed operating cash, capital
spending, a revolver repayment, tax payments, and leases, but publishes no dated
roll-forward. Nintendo reports JPY673.4 billion of headroom above a JPY500 billion
operating floor after a two-year stress and names negative operating cash,
facilities spending, leases, and dividends, likewise without a dated
roll-forward.[3][4]

Value investors and company-research agents benefit if a survival classification
can be challenged without access to deleted working records. The question is not
whether every calculation notebook should be published. It is whether the
reported inputs preserve enough timing, cash-availability, and no-double-counting
evidence to reproduce the first liquidity trough that supports `Watch` rather
than `Fragile`, `Unclear`, or another conclusion. This is direct ANCHOR Path B
work on repeatable risk and financial-health analysis, not a security
recommendation or an Investing Hub edit.[1]

After the current reports and full contract were inspected, the provisional
hypothesis was that a compact dated bridge - opening usable cash, stressed cash
inflow, accessible funding, mandatory uses, closing cash, and headroom at each
material date - could expose an omitted obligation, timing mismatch, or duplicate
cash use that a single terminal headroom number cannot. A credible alternative is
that each report's narrative plus cited filings already permits an unambiguous
independent reconstruction and the existing framework already blocks any missing
input, so another output rule would add burden without changing a decision. The
idea is not worth pursuing if both figures reproduce under one supported timeline,
if the full current contract reaches the same classifications, or if the bridge
changes no decision-relevant field.[2][5][6]

## Research Plan

Run one frozen, read-only comparison. Do not edit Investing Hub, its reports,
frameworks, template, source data, or portfolio records during research.

1. Freeze the exact financial-health framework, company template, Crocs and
   Nintendo reports, their cited issuer records, valuation dates, units, cash
   perimeters, stress horizons, and current financial-health and thesis labels.
2. Before constructing a candidate display, have two separate readers apply the
   full current framework and template to each report. Preserve their separate
   classifications and reasons. Do not replace the semantic baseline with a
   checklist that asks only whether a bridge table is present.
3. Independently reconstruct the Crocs stress from dated opening usable cash,
   stressed operating cash, capital spending, lease principal, revolver and debt
   maturities, tax payments, facility availability, minimum operating cash, and
   any other mandatory use supported by the cited filings. Reproduce or reject
   the reported USD69 million headroom and identify the first minimum date.
4. Independently reconstruct the Nintendo stress on the same principles,
   separating cash and securities by availability and applying the reported
   operating-cash shocks, facilities spending, dividends, leases, and JPY500
   billion operating floor. Reproduce or reject the reported JPY673.4 billion
   headroom and identify the first minimum date.
5. Freeze one compact candidate bridge only after the reconstructions are defined.
   Use one row per material date and explicit columns for opening usable cash,
   stressed cash generation, accessible committed funding, mandatory outflows,
   closing usable cash, floor, and headroom. Every input must carry a source or an
   assumption label; unavailable timing remains unknown rather than being spread
   evenly without disclosure.
6. Compare the original reports, the full current contract, and the candidate.
   Record changes in headroom, first-shortfall date, financial-health area and
   overall classification, confidence, thesis status, final verdict, and review
   trigger. A clearer display without a changed supported field is not by itself
   decision value.
7. Compare doing nothing, a report-only correction, a retained calculation record,
   one compact report bridge, and a framework or template amendment. Prefer the
   smallest reversible option and reject a mandatory companion file if the report
   can remain independently checkable without it.

Research supports a proposal only if both published baselines are first assessed
under the full current contract and the compact bridge uniquely exposes or
prevents a decision-relevant error: an unreproduced amount, omitted mandatory use,
wrong first-shortfall date, inconsistent funding assumption, double count, or a
changed classification, confidence, thesis, or final-verdict field. Stop without
a proposal if both stress results reproduce from published evidence, if the
current contract already forces the same correction, if missing dates make every
candidate bridge arbitrary, or if the only effect is a larger report.[2][5][6][8]

Confidence is low. The framework and two reports establish concrete numerical
claims and a visible output boundary, while independent finance knowledge supports
dated, linked liquidity paths.[2][3][4][5][6] Confidence remains low because only
two company reports exist, the original working records are unavailable, the
issuer-source reconstructions have not been performed, and the full current
contract may already be sufficient. Confidence would rise if independent
reconstructions reveal a unique, source-pinned decision change. It would fall if
both headroom figures and labels reproduce without ambiguity or if a compact
bridge adds no detection over the current semantic gate.
